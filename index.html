About the data. Release dates, Rotten Tomatoes scores and on-sale status were checked on 26 Oct 2026. The 3-day ticket outlook is worked out from those release and sale dates; exact showtimes and seat maps aren't published openly, so each "Book" button opens Fandango where you pick the seat.
